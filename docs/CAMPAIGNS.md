# Campaigns: a short guide

For the people who make the banners. The signatures themselves are made by IT; a campaign is a
banner that goes into them for some days, and out again by itself.

## A campaign in five steps

1. **Campaigns** (in the sidebar) → **+ New campaign**. Or **From an earlier one…**: last year's,
   from today on, its banner and rules again. **Copy for next year** does the same for next year.
2. **Name, From, Until.** *Weekdays* only when it is for some days of the week (a webinar on
   Thursdays); none ticked is every day.
3. **The banner**: *Image with link*, *Text with button*, *Image and text*, or *HTML of your own*.
   - The image: *Upload…* (PNG or JPG). It is made smaller by itself (1200 pixels wide at most).
   - *Width*: 400 to 600 pixels. Wider is too wide on phones.
   - *Alt text*: what the image says, for those whose mail program blocks images. Always fill it in.
   - *Link*: an `https://` address. *Link tracking* adds `utm_campaign=<name>`, so the web
     statistics show the visits that came from mails.
   - *Colour*: the house colours (IT keeps them in the editor).
   - *+ Language*: the banner again in English, French or Italian, for those whose Microsoft 365
     language is that one.
   - *+ Banner in turns*: several banners that take turns, mail by mail.
   The preview below shows it under a real signature.
4. **Where and who**: all signatures or some; only in new mail (not in every reply of a long
   thread); groups or departments, or everyone.
5. **Publish…**. It lists what would go wrong (no alt text, too wide, a link without https, two
   campaigns at the same time) before anything goes out. A minute or two later, new mails have it.

## Check before it starts

- **On a day** (top right): a date, and what each signature gets then. With **For a person**: that
  person's signature for new mail and for replies on that day, with which banner.
- The status bar says when a campaign ends in three days and none follows.

## Good to know

- **Images**: PNG for logos and text, JPG for photos; under 100 KB. A transparent dark logo
  disappears in dark mode: give it a light background.
- **Classic Outlook** keeps the signature it puts in by itself in the mailbox, about 30 KB. A heavy
  banner there goes without its image (the preview and *Publish…* say so). Smaller is better.
- **Rounded corners** do not show in classic Outlook for Windows.
- **Where the banner goes** in a signature is the orange *Campaign banner* place; without it,
  below the signature. Moving that place is for IT (it is part of the signature).
- With the campaign role you change only campaigns and their images; the signatures stay as IT made
  them, and *Publish* sends your campaigns with what is published already.
